# Clean Scene Project

Build a polished, professional, mobile-responsive website for my commercial cleaning company:

BUSINESS NAME:

Clean Scene Cleaning Services LLC

BUSINESS TYPE:

Commercial cleaning company serving North Central Indiana.

IMPORTANT BRAND POSITIONING:

Clean Scene specializes in professional cleaning for businesses and commercial properties. The website should NOT look like a residential house-cleaning or maid-service website.

Our primary target clients include:

- Offices

- Professional/business spaces

- Lobbies and common areas

- Small-to-medium commercial facilities

- Daycares

- Dental/medical-style professional offices

- Move-in/move-out commercial spaces

Do not emphasize residential cleaning.

BRANDING:

I will upload my official Clean Scene logo and banner images. USE MY UPLOADED LOGO EXACTLY AS PROVIDED. Do not redraw, recreate, modify, simplify, recolor, or generate a replacement logo.

Use the visual identity from my uploaded branding throughout the website:

- Deep forest green

- Soft muted blue

- Sage/leaf green

- Warm cream/off-white

- Very small amounts of charcoal/dark gray

The overall aesthetic should feel:

- Premium

- Professional

- Calm

- Clean

- Modern

- Trustworthy

- Established

- Commercial/corporate

- Not overly flashy

- Not like a generic AI-generated cleaning website

Use high-quality imagery of modern commercial interiors such as offices, glass-walled workspaces, professional lobbies, reception areas, conference rooms, hallways and common areas.

Avoid cliché residential-cleaning imagery such as houses, kitchens, bedrooms, families, maids, feather dusters, rubber gloves, cartoon bubbles, or people dramatically spraying counters.

TYPOGRAPHY:

Use an elegant serif font for important headings when appropriate, paired with a clean modern sans-serif font for body copy. Match the sophisticated style of my existing branding.
